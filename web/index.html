QPredict

Seal a prediction nobody can peek at — not even you. The chain settles it.

How it works

Pick EVEN or ODD. Your guess is hashed with a random salt in your browser and only the hash goes on-chain. The contract mixes that hash with the block your prediction lands in — its number, timestamp and proposer, none of which you can know when you sign — and fixes the outcome. Reveal your guess afterwards and the contract checks it against the seal and records the result forever. Leave it unrevealed too long and anyone can forfeit it as a loss: a sealed prediction can never be quietly buried.
